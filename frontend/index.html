About ContactHub

ContactHub is a clean, minimal contact directory designed to organize your professional connections without unnecessary clutter. It is built with simplicity at its core, focusing on speed and a clean visual layout.

Key Features

  • Sleek Interface: A high-contrast grid system designed for effortless reading, with full native dark-mode integration.
  • Instant Search: Find any profile in your directory instantly by searching names, emails, companies, or roles.
  • Format Guards: Smart form validation automatically blocks duplicate emails and malformed phone numbers.
  • Cloud Database: Securely synchronizes your database, ensuring your networking contacts are safe.
